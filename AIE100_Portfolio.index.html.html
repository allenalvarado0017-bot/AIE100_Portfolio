Featured Entry • AIE100 Reflection Academic Year 2026 • Mapúa University

Data Literacy, Model Failures, & The Humility of AI Engineering

"Studying AI engineering has taught me that building intelligent systems is less about chasing perfect metrics and more about mastering data literacy and humility..."

While training a recent model to near-perfect accuracy on clean datasets, I watched it completely fall apart when exposed to the messy chaos of real-world data. This failure forced me to look past the code and confront the "black box" of machine learning, revealing that an engineer's true responsibility lies in deeply questioning data biases and prioritizing model explainability.

Moving forward, I no longer view AI as a magic tool, but as a discipline where success requires spending more time cleaning data and designing transparent pipelines than simply tuning algorithms.
